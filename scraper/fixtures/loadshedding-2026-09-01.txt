BEL issues emergency load shedding schedule following CFE power curtailment alert
Posted:
Tuesday, September 1, 2026
3:05 pm
By Zoila Palma:
Belize Electricity Limited (BEL) has issued an emergency customer notice warning of potential load shedding today, Tuesday, September 1, 2026.
The tentative power disruptions come after Mexico's Comisión Federal de Electricidad (CFE) notified BEL that high electricity demand on the Mexican grid may force them to curtail energy supply to Belize.
BEL stressed that because load shedding schedules depend on real-time energy demand and generation projections, the timetable remains tentative and subject to quick changes.
Tentative Outage Schedule for Tuesday, September 1, 2026
Orange Walk District
Time: 6:00 PM – 9:00 PM
Feeders & Zones: Feeder 2 (Zone: All)
Areas Affected: Portions of Orange Walk Town stretching from the Philip Goldson Highway to Guadalupe Street, Temash Street to Liberty Avenue, the Louisiana Area to Riverside Street, Bakers to San Antonio Road, Dunn Street to Cinderella Street, Queen Victoria Avenue to Otro Benque Road, Main Street, and Pettville to San Estevan Village.
Ladyville & Old Northern Highway Communities
Time: 6:00 PM – 9:00 PM
Feeders & Zones: Feeder 2 (Zone: All)
Areas Affected: All areas along the Philip Goldson Highway from Courtenay Estate to Maskall, including Ladyville, Lords Bank, Lake Garden, Los Lagos, Sandhill, Gardenia, Biscayne, Rhaburn Ridge, Crooked Tree, and all surrounding communities along the Old Northern Highway.
Belmopan & Surrounding Villages
Time: 9:00 PM – 11:59 PM
Feeders & Zones: Feeder 4 (Zone: All)
Areas Affected: Portions of Belmopan including University Heights, Maya Mopan, Las Flores, J&W, and the Rivera area; communities along Miles 31 to 50 on the George Price Highway including Mahogany Heights, St. Matthews, Harmonyville, More Tomorrow, Cotton Tree, Roaring Creek, Valley of Peace, Camalote, Teakettle, Ontario, Buena Vista, San Marcus, Santa Teresita, Selena, and Los Tambos; as well as areas along the Hummingbird Highway from Belmopan to Armenia.
