About this site

A free map that answers one question: is the power going off in my area, and when? It collects Belize Electricity Limited's public outage notices and load-shedding schedules and puts them on a map of Belize you can search by village, town or street.

Check my area

What it does

  • Planned and unscheduled outages from BEL's Power Updates page.
  • Load shedding, the rolling blackouts BEL runs when there is not enough power to go round. BEL announces these on Facebook, so this site gathers them from Belize news reports instead. They are always tentative.
  • Search for your village, town or street. The map moves there and tells you whether anything BEL has published applies to it.

How to read an answer

  • Off now
  • Off later today
  • Scheduled for another day
  • Nothing listed
  • If BEL named your place in a notice, you get a definite answer.
  • If BEL only named places near you, you see "Your street is not named" and the places each notice does name, so you can judge for yourself.
  • Load shedding always reads "may be off", because BEL can change the schedule at short notice.

What is a feeder?

A feeder is one of the power lines running out of a substation. BEL switches power off one feeder at a time, so its notices name feeders rather than streets.

BEL does not publish which feeder serves which street, so the shapes on the map are drawn around the places BEL has named in its own notices. They show roughly where a feeder reaches, not its exact edge.

Where the data comes from

  • Outages: bel.com.bz/PowerUpdates
  • Load shedding: Breaking Belize News and Love FM, which republish BEL's schedules
  • Map: OpenStreetMap contributors for streets, roads and town outlines, GeoNames for place names, and geoBoundaries for district lines

The page shows when the data was last checked. If that was more than 12 hours ago, a warning appears at the top.

What it cannot do

  • It cannot tell you for certain which feeder your street is on.
  • It only knows what BEL has published. A fault BEL has not announced will not appear.
  • Town outlines cover about 110 settlements. Places without one show as a dot.

Privacy

No cookies, no tracking and no accounts. The page does not send your searches anywhere and makes no requests to other websites.

Not affiliated with Belize Electricity Limited. Compiled from public notices. Always check the BEL 24-7 app before relying on it.
